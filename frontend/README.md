# Civic Copilot - AI Complaint Management for Indian Cities

> Citizens report. AI understands. Cities respond.

## What it does differently
- **Understands natural language** - citizens describe issues in their own words, AI extracts structure
- **Sees what you see** - computer vision detects and scores issues from photos automatically
- **Predicts before problems peak** - ML engine identifies areas at risk before they become critical
